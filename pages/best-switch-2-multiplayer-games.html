Best Multiplayer Nintendo Switch 2 Games (2026)

The Switch 2 is the best multiplayer console Nintendo has ever made — local co-op, online play, and party games that work whether you have one TV or four friends in the same room. Here's what's worth playing.

Why Switch 2 is the best console for multiplayer

Every Switch 2 comes with two Joy-Cons that separate into individual controllers — you have a two-player setup the moment you open the box. No buying a second controller before you can play with someone else. No split-screen that feels like a compromise. The Switch 2 is designed from the ground up for shared play, and the library reflects that.

The hybrid design also means multiplayer happens naturally: dock it to the TV for a living room session, take it handheld for a long-distance co-op game via online, or tilt the screen on its kickstand and play tabletop-style with a friend on a plane. No other console offers all three without accessories.

It Takes Two

EA / Hazelight · Rated T · Co-op Adventure · 2 players only · local or online

Best Co-op Game on Switch 2
2 players required Local or online 10–12 hours Award-winning Rated T

It Takes Two won Game of the Year for a reason — it's the most inventively designed co-op game ever made and it only works with two players, which means it demands genuine collaboration in a way most co-op games don't. Every chapter introduces a completely new gameplay mechanic that both players must master together — one chapter has one player controlling time while the other controls space; another has one player as a nail and one as a hammer. No two chapters feel alike.

The Friend's Pass means only one copy is needed — the second player downloads a free trial version and both play together, either on the same couch or online. For couples, siblings, or close friends looking for a shared gaming experience with narrative depth, nothing on the Switch 2 comes close.

Super Mario Bros. Wonder

Nintendo · Rated E · 2D Platformer · 1–4 players local

Best 4-Player Co-op
Up to 4 players Local only All skill levels Constantly surprising Rated E

Four-player simultaneous platforming is notoriously hard to get right — too much chaos makes it unplayable. Wonder solves this beautifully by giving each player enough screen presence and individual agency that four people can play together without feeling like they're fighting over the same space. The Wonder Flower sequences that transform each level create shared "did that just happen" moments that four players experience together.

The ability-assist characters (Nabbit and Yoshi variants) can't take damage, making it accessible for mixed skill groups. One experienced player can carry the level while newer players explore freely. The best local four-player platformer on the platform.

Overcooked 2

Team17 · Rated E · Co-op Cooking · 1–4 players local or online

Best Hidden Gem
1–4 players Local + online Communication required Chaotic fun Friendship tester

Overcooked 2 is the best relationship-testing game ever made — in the best possible way. Players work together in increasingly chaotic kitchen environments, chopping ingredients, cooking dishes, and plating orders under time pressure while the kitchen itself changes around them. The coordination required to succeed is genuinely difficult and the failure to coordinate is genuinely hilarious.

It's the game most likely to produce moments that become stories — "remember when we were cooking on two moving ships and you threw the soup at me" is a sentence Overcooked generates regularly. Throw It Too adds food-throwing mechanics that escalate the chaos further. Essential purchase for anyone who plays with roommates, partners, or friends.

💎 Hidden gem pick: Overcooked 2 is consistently underrated because it sounds simple — it's a cooking game. It's actually one of the most skillfully designed co-op experiences on any platform. Don't skip it based on the premise.

Lovers in a Dangerous Spacetime

Asteroid Base · Rated E10+ · Co-op Shooter · 1–4 players local

Hidden Gem
1–4 players Local only Beautiful art style Space shooter $15 eShop

One of the most criminally underplayed games on Nintendo's platform. Players crew a spaceship together — each person operates a different gun, engine, or shield station while navigating through neon-colored space levels rescuing animals from the Anti-Love forces. The strategy of who covers which station, when to boost, and when to divert power creates natural teamwork moments that feel genuinely earned.

At $15 on the eShop it's the best value multiplayer game on this list. The art style is gorgeous, the difficulty scales well for 1–4 players, and it has a warmth and originality that bigger-budget games often lack. If you've never heard of it, that's exactly why it's on this list.

💎 Hidden gem pick: $15 eShop purchase. No physical version. One of the most warmly designed co-op games Nintendo's platform has hosted — passed over by most buyers purely because it has no marketing budget.

Mario Kart World

Nintendo · Rated E · Racing · 1–4 players local · online · Switch 2 exclusive

Best Competitive Local
4-player local Online multiplayer Switch 2 exclusive Open world between races All skill levels

The definitive Switch 2 multiplayer experience. Mario Kart World builds on the foundations of MK8 Deluxe — the smoothest, best-balanced kart racer ever made — and adds an open world connecting all the tracks, a massive new roster, and Switch 2-specific features that leverage the new hardware's power. Four players on one screen with stable 60fps is the standard here, not a compromise.

The item system is perfectly tuned for mixed-skill groups: skilled players race faster but items give lower-placed racers a fighting chance. A blue shell at the right moment creates a memory. The skill ceiling is genuinely high for competitive players who want to get fast, while remaining immediately accessible for someone picking up a controller for the first time.

Super Smash Bros. Ultimate

Nintendo · Rated E10+ · Fighter · 1–8 players local · online

Best Fighting Game

Super Smash Bros. Ultimate is the largest fighting game ever made — 80+ fighters, dozens of stages, and a competitive depth that professional players have spent years mastering. Splatoon 3

Nintendo · Rated E10+ · Online Shooter · 1–8 players online · local 2

Best Online Game
Online 4v4 Local 2-player Competitive ranked Regular updates Switch 2 enhanced

Splatoon is Nintendo's most original multiplayer franchise — a team-based shooter where the goal is covering the floor in your color ink rather than eliminating opponents. The mechanic shift from killing to painting makes it accessible to players who find conventional shooters stressful, while the competitive depth of movement, weapon types, and team composition gives dedicated players a high skill ceiling.

Splatoon 3 has the largest content base of any game in the series, with regular Splatfest events, a full single-player campaign, and a cooperative Salmon Run mode where up to four players defend against waves of enemies. On Switch 2 it runs noticeably smoother with faster online matchmaking. The active player base remains strong years after launch.

What you need for multiplayer

  • 2 players local: The included Joy-Cons used sideways. No extra purchases needed.
  • 3–4 players local: An additional Joy-Con pair ($70–$80) or two pro controllers. One extra Joy-Con set handles four players across most games.
  • Online multiplayer: Nintendo Switch Online subscription ($20/year individual, $35/year family plan). Required for any online play across all Switch 2 games.
  • Jackbox specifically: Each player uses their smartphone. One Switch 2 and one TV is all you need for up to 10 players.

Frequently asked questions

Can you play Switch 2 multiplayer without extra controllers?
Yes — for two players. The included Joy-Cons separate and work as two individual controllers held sideways for local multiplayer in most games. For 3–4 players you'll need additional controllers. For online play, one person uses the full console normally while others connect via their own consoles.

Which Switch 2 games support couch co-op?
Mario Kart World, Super Mario Bros. Wonder, Super Mario Party Jamboree, Nintendo Switch Sports, Overcooked 2, and Super Smash Bros. Ultimate all support 2–4 player local co-op or competitive play on one console. It Takes Two supports exactly 2 players locally.

Do you need Nintendo Switch Online for local multiplayer?
No — local multiplayer (same room, same TV) doesn't require Nintendo Switch Online. NSO is only required for online play over the internet. You can play Mario Kart World with four people on one TV without any subscription.

What's the best multiplayer game for non-gamers?
Nintendo Switch Sports or Jackbox Party Pack. Switch Sports uses motion controls that don't require button knowledge. Jackbox uses phones as controllers, removing the "I don't know how to use a controller" barrier entirely.

Can you play Switch 2 multiplayer online with Switch 1 players?
For most games, yes — online multiplayer is cross-compatible between Switch 1 and Switch 2 players in the same game. Switch 2 exclusive titles obviously can't be played by Switch 1 owners since those games don't exist on that hardware.
