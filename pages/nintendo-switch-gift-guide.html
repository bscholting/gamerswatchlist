Frequently asked questions

Can I gift digital games on Nintendo Switch 2?
Not directly — the Nintendo eShop doesn't support gifting specific games to another person's account. The best way to give a digital game is through a Nintendo eShop gift card in the appropriate amount. The recipient redeems it and buys whatever they want, including the game you had in mind.

What if I don't know whether they have Switch 2 or Switch 1?
Gift a physical game rather than Switch 2-exclusive hardware. Every game on this list marked "Switch 2 backward compatible" works on both consoles. The safest game gifts across both platforms: Stardew Valley, Animal Crossing, Mario Kart 8 Deluxe (not World), Super Mario Bros. Wonder, or an eShop card.

What's the best gift for someone getting a Switch 2 for the first time?
Buy the Switch 2 + Mario Kart World bundle if budget allows. Then add a screen protector and carrying case as supplementary gifts — every new Switch 2 owner needs both immediately. If buying only accessories to go with hardware someone else is giving: the amFilm screen protector + Tomtoc case + one game they'd enjoy is a perfect supporting gift set.

Do I need to buy Switch 2 games specifically, or will old Switch games work?
Old Switch games work on Switch 2 — full backward compatibility. You don't need to buy Switch 2-exclusive titles. Games marked "Switch 2 exclusive" (Mario Kart World) require Switch 2 hardware. Everything else works on both consoles.
