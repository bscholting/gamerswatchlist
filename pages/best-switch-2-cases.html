⚠ Switch 1 cases do not fit Switch 2

The Switch 2 is larger than the original Switch and Switch OLED — longer, wider, and slightly thicker. Any case designed for Switch 1 or OLED will be too small to fit. Always check that a case explicitly states Switch 2 compatibility before buying.

Hard shell vs soft case — which should you buy?

This is the first decision to make. Both have genuine trade-offs and the right answer depends on how you carry your Switch 2.

🛡️

Hard shell — best for most people

A rigid EVA or polycarbonate exterior that protects against drops, compression, and impact. Won't crumple if something heavy lands on top of it in a bag.

  • Drop and crush protection
  • Rigid structure protects screen
  • Usually water-resistant exterior
  • Holds its shape in a bag
  • Bulkier than soft cases
  • Less capacity for accessories
👜

Soft case — best for light carry

Fabric or neoprene construction that's lighter and more flexible. Good for protecting against scratches and minor bumps, not drops or compression.

  • Lighter and slimmer profile
  • More pockets and organization
  • Easier to fit in tight spaces
  • Often cheaper
  • No drop protection
  • Can be crushed in a full bag
💡 Our recommendation: Unless you're only ever carrying the Switch 2 on its own from couch to desk, buy a hard shell. The Switch 2's screen is large and valuable — a hard shell that resists compression when your backpack gets stuffed is worth the extra bulk.

What to look for in a Switch 2 case

  • Switch 2 specific fit — not labeled for Switch 1, OLED, or "all models." Custom-fit cases protect better and feel less sloppy.
  • Game card storage — at minimum 8 slots, ideally 12–16 if you buy physical games. Digital-only players can ignore this.
  • Microfiber or soft interior lining — protects the screen from scratches. Avoid any case with an abrasive interior.
  • Charging cable pass-through or cable pouch — if you travel, you need somewhere to put the USB-C cable.
  • Space for the dock or Joy-Con grip — most slim cases don't include this; travel cases and large kits typically do.

What about cases that fit the dock?

Most slim carrying cases don't have room for the Switch 2 dock — it's simply too large. If you need a bag that carries the full setup (console, dock, cables, Joy-Con grip, and games), you're looking at a dedicated travel bag rather than a carrying case.

The best option for this is a medium-sized tech organizer bag like the Tomtoc Tech Organizer Pouch or the Bagsmart Electronic Organizer — neither is Switch 2-specific but both fit the dock, multiple cables, a power bank, and the slim case containing the console. Search "Nintendo Switch 2 travel bag" on Amazon for options that package the whole setup together.

Caring for your Switch 2 case

Hard shell EVA cases can be wiped clean with a slightly damp cloth. Don't submerge them — water-resistant means splash-proof, not waterproof. For fabric and neoprene soft cases, spot-clean with mild soap and air dry completely before putting the Switch 2 back in. Never machine wash — it warps the structure.

Frequently asked questions

Will my Switch 1 case fit Switch 2?
No. The Switch 2 is physically larger than the original Switch and Switch OLED. Switch 1 cases are too small — the console either won't fit or will be forced in with pressure on the screen. Buy a Switch 2-specific case.

Should I keep the Joy-Cons attached when storing in the case?
Yes for most cases — Switch 2 cases are sized for the console with Joy-Cons attached. Removing the Joy-Cons to store separately creates a loose fit and the console can rattle. Cases designed for console-only storage are less common.

How many game card slots do I need?
8 slots covers most physical libraries comfortably. If you buy a lot of physical games, go for 12–16. Digital-only players can ignore game slots entirely and prioritize accessory pockets for cables and a power bank instead.

Are officially licensed cases better than third-party?
Not necessarily. HORI is excellent because they have genuine quality standards, but most of the best cases (Tomtoc, Orzly) are third-party. The Nintendo license guarantees a correct fit but doesn't guarantee build quality. Check reviews regardless of licensing.

Can I fit a screen protector on the Switch 2 and still use a case?
Yes — a screen protector adds less than 0.5mm of thickness and all the cases on this list accommodate it easily. Apply the screen protector first, then test the case fit.
