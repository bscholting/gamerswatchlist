How to Transfer Data from Switch to Switch 2

Everything — your save files, screenshots, game library, and microSD card — transfers to Switch 2. Here's the complete step-by-step process. Takes about 20 minutes.

⚡ What transfers over
  • All save data (for games that support it)
  • Your entire Nintendo eShop digital game library
  • Screenshots and video captures
  • User profiles and settings
  • microSD card data (games, DLC, updates)
  • Nintendo Switch Online saves (via cloud)

Before you start — what you'll need

Get these ready before beginning the transfer. Having everything on hand means no interruptions mid-process.

Requirements checklist
✓

Both consoles charged

Keep both plugged into power during transfer — a dead battery mid-transfer can corrupt data.

✓

Both consoles connected to Wi-Fi

Same network is ideal but not required. A 5GHz network speeds things up significantly.

✓

Nintendo Account login details

You'll sign into your Nintendo Account on Switch 2 to restore your digital library.

✓

About 20–30 minutes

The actual transfer is fast. Downloading your digital game library takes longer depending on your internet speed.

📌 Don't trade in your Switch 1 yet. Complete the full transfer process and verify everything is working on Switch 2 before selling or trading your old console. Some save data isn't covered by cloud backup and requires the local transfer.

Method 1: Console transfer tool (recommended)

This is Nintendo's built-in transfer system. It moves your user profile, save data, screenshots, and settings directly from Switch 1 to Switch 2 over your local network. This is the method to use for most people.

Step-by-step — console transfer tool
1

Power on your Switch 2 and begin setup

When you first power on a new Switch 2, the setup wizard asks if you want to transfer data from another console. Select Transfer Data from Nintendo Switch. If you've already completed initial setup, go to System Settings → System → Transfer Your Save Data.

2

On your Switch 1, open System Settings

Go to System Settings → System → Transfer Your Save Data on the source console. Select Send Save Data to Another Console.

Switch 1 path: HOME → System Settings → System → Transfer Your Save Data
3

Select your user profile

Choose the user profile you want to transfer. If you have multiple users on the Switch 1, you'll need to run the transfer once per user.

4

Choose what to transfer

You can transfer all save data at once or select individual games. Transferring everything is usually the right call — you can always delete saves you don't need later.

5

Connect the two consoles

Both consoles will display a code or QR-style connection screen. Follow the on-screen prompts to establish a direct local connection between them. They don't need to be on the same Wi-Fi — they connect directly.

6

Wait for the transfer to complete

Transfer time depends on how much save data you have. For most people, this takes 5–15 minutes. Keep both consoles powered and nearby — don't let them sleep.

⚠ Do not turn off either console during transfer
7

Verify the transfer on Switch 2

Once complete, check a few games on Switch 2 to confirm saves transferred correctly. Load the game and verify your progress is intact before moving on.

Method 2: Nintendo Switch Online cloud saves

If you have a Nintendo Switch Online subscription, most games automatically back up save data to Nintendo's servers. This is your safety net — and for some people, all they need.

● Best for: NSO subscribers with a standard game library

On your Switch 2, sign into your Nintendo Account. Go to System Settings → Data Management → Save Data Cloud. Download your cloud saves for each game. This works for most first-party Nintendo titles and many third-party games. Check each game's save data settings to confirm cloud backup is enabled.

⚠ Not all games support cloud saves. A handful of titles — some Animal Crossing: New Horizons island data, certain third-party games — don't support cloud backup for anti-cheat or design reasons. For those titles, the local console transfer (Method 1) is the only way to move saves. Check Nintendo's list of games without cloud save support →

Transferring your digital game library

Your digital games aren't stored on the console — they're tied to your Nintendo Account. This makes the transfer effortless:

Restoring your digital library on Switch 2
1

Sign into your Nintendo Account on Switch 2

During initial setup or via System Settings → Users → Add User. Use the same Nintendo Account linked to your Switch 1 purchases.

2

Open the Nintendo eShop

Go to your account page (top-right icon) → Redownload. Every game you've ever purchased appears here.

3

Download the games you want

You don't have to download everything immediately — games download in the background while you play. Download your most-played games first and let the rest come over time.

Tip: A 512GB microSD card means you won't have to manage storage constantly

Transferring your microSD card

If you use a microSD card in your Switch 1 for extra game storage, the good news is simple: the card works in Switch 2 without any reformatting or conversion. Remove it from your Switch 1 and insert it into Switch 2's microSD slot. Your downloaded games, DLC, and updates stored on the card are immediately accessible.

💡 This is a good time to upgrade your microSD card. Switch 2 games are significantly larger than Switch 1 titles — some first-party Switch 2 games exceed 30GB. If you've been getting by on a 64GB or 128GB card, upgrading to 256GB or 512GB now means you won't hit a storage wall six months in.

Transferring screenshots and videos

Screenshots and video clips captured on Switch 1 don't move automatically via the console transfer tool — they live in the Album and require a separate step to preserve.

Two ways to save your screenshots

Via microSD card

If your screenshots are stored on a microSD card (Album → Storage: microSD), the card transfer above moves them automatically. Check Album settings to confirm storage location.

Via USB cable to PC

Connect Switch 1 to a PC via USB-C. Go to System Settings → Data Management → Manage Screenshots and Videos → Copy to a Computer via USB Connection. Copy the folder to your computer, then move to Switch 2 the same way.

What doesn't transfer

A few things stay on Switch 1 or require special handling:

  • Animal Crossing: New Horizons island data — requires Nintendo's specific island transfer tool, separate from the general save transfer. Search "Animal Crossing Switch 2 island transfer" for Nintendo's dedicated guide.
  • Local user accounts not linked to a Nintendo Account — if you have a local account (no Nintendo Account attached), create and link a Nintendo Account before transferring.
  • Games without cloud save support — must use the local transfer tool (Method 1). Cannot be recovered after the fact if you skip this step.
  • Downloaded demos — these don't carry over, but you can re-download them for free from the eShop.

Frequently asked questions

Can I keep playing on Switch 1 after the transfer?
Yes — the transfer copies your data, it doesn't delete it from Switch 1. However, saves made on Switch 1 after the transfer won't automatically sync to Switch 2. Pick one console as your primary and stick with it to avoid conflicting saves.

Do I need Nintendo Switch Online to transfer?
No. The local console transfer tool (Method 1) works without an NSO subscription. NSO just adds the convenience of cloud saves as a backup method.

How long does the full transfer take?
The save data transfer itself takes 5–20 minutes depending on library size. Redownloading your full digital game library takes much longer — budget a few hours for that on a fast connection, or let it run overnight.

What if I already set up Switch 2 without transferring?
You can still run the transfer tool at any time via System Settings on both consoles. It's not limited to initial setup.

Will my Nintendo Switch Online subscription work on Switch 2?
Yes. Your NSO subscription is tied to your Nintendo Account, not the hardware. Sign into the same account on Switch 2 and your subscription is active immediately.

Do physical game cartridges need to be transferred?
No — physical cartridges work in Switch 2 directly. Just insert the card. Only the save data associated with that game needs to be transferred.
