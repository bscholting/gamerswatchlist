Will there be Nintendo Switch 2 deals on Black Friday 2026?

Yes — with caveats. The Switch 2 launched in 2025, making Black Friday 2026 its first full holiday season as a maturing platform. Based on Nintendo’s historical pricing behavior and current retailer signals, here’s what to realistically expect.

How to avoid fake Black Friday deals

  • Check CamelCamelCamel.com before buying anything on Amazon. This free tool shows the complete price history of any Amazon product. If something is “40% off” but has been that low multiple times before, it’s not a deal.
  • Ignore percentage-off claims without knowing the real retail price. Focus on absolute dollar savings rather than percentages.
  • Nintendo first-party game prices are publicly known. Any deal claiming $25 off a game that normally sells for $59.99 at major retailers is likely real. Any claiming $50 off the same game is misrepresenting the original price.
  • Be skeptical of marketplace sellers on Amazon. “Fulfilled by Amazon” is fine. Third-party marketplace sellers offering Nintendo hardware at suspiciously low prices should be avoided.
