Privacy Policy

We've written this in plain English because legal boilerplate that nobody reads doesn't actually respect your privacy — it just checks a box. Here's exactly what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

⚡ The short version

Gamers Watchlist collects minimal data. We don't sell your information to anyone. We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used (page views, traffic sources — nothing personal). If you subscribe to our newsletter, we store your email address to send the newsletter and nothing else. Who we are

Gamers Watchlist (gamerswatchlist.com) is a Nintendo Switch review and deals site. It is operated as an independent website. When this policy says "we," "us," or "our," it means Gamers Watchlist and its owner.

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at hello@gamerswatchlist.com.

What data we collect — and what we don't

We collect the minimum information needed to run the site and send the newsletter. Here's the full picture:

📊

Analytics data (via Google Analytics)

Page views, general location (country/city level), device type, browser, traffic source, and time on page. This is aggregated and anonymous — we see "500 people visited from the US on mobile" not "John Smith visited from Chicago." Used to understand which content is helpful and how the site is used.

📧

Email address (newsletter subscribers only)

If you subscribe to the weekly deals newsletter, we store your email address to send you the newsletter. That's the only thing we use it for. We don't send promotional emails from third parties, we don't sell your email, and we don't share it with anyone outside of our email service provider.

✉️

Contact emails

If you email us at our contact address, we receive and read your message. We don't add contact emails to any list or use them for anything other than responding to your message.

🚫

What we don't collect

We don't collect your name, physical address, phone number, payment information, or any account information. We don't build user profiles, track you across other websites, or use advertising networks that do so.

Google Analytics

Gamers Watchlist uses Google Analytics to understand how the site is used. Google Analytics collects information including which pages you visit, how long you spend on them, what device and browser you're using, and general geographic information (country and city — not your street address).

This data is processed by Google and shared with us in aggregated, anonymized form. We use it to understand which articles are most helpful, where our readers come from, and how the site performs — nothing more.

IP anonymization is enabled on our Google Analytics implementation, which means your full IP address is never stored by Google on our behalf.

Google's own privacy policy governs how they handle analytics data. You can read it at policies.google.com/privacy. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by websites you visit. Here's what cookies Gamers Watchlist uses:

📊

Google Analytics cookies

These cookies (typically named _ga, _gid, _gat) help Google Analytics distinguish between unique visitors and track session information. They contain a randomly generated identifier — not your personal information. They expire after 2 years (_ga), 24 hours (_gid), or 1 minute (_gat).

🔗

Affiliate tracking cookies

When you click an affiliate link to Amazon, Best Buy, or another retailer, that retailer may set a cookie on your device to track that the referral came from this site. This is how affiliate commissions are attributed. These cookies are set by the retailer, not by us, and governed by the retailer's own privacy policy.

✅

No advertising or tracking cookies from us

Gamers Watchlist does not use advertising networks, remarketing pixels, or third-party tracking cookies beyond the Google Analytics cookies described above. We don't show ads, so we don't need the tracking infrastructure that comes with advertising.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to view, block, or delete cookies. Blocking all cookies will affect site functionality across the web — to opt out of Google Analytics specifically without blocking other cookies, use the browser add-on linked in the Analytics section above.

Newsletter and email

If you subscribe to the Gamers Watchlist weekly deals newsletter, here's exactly how we handle your email:

  • What we store: Your email address and the date you subscribed.
  • What we send: One email per week (every Thursday) covering Nintendo Switch deals, eShop sales, and accessory price drops. Occasional additional emails when a significant deal or announcement warrants it — but we aim to keep this rare.
  • Who processes it: Email delivery is handled by Beehiiv, our email service provider. Your email address is stored on their platform. Beehiiv's privacy policy applies to how they handle the data on their end — you can read it at beehiiv.com.
  • Who we share it with: Nobody. We do not sell, rent, or share subscriber email addresses with any third party for any purpose.
  • How to unsubscribe: Every newsletter email contains a one-click unsubscribe link at the bottom. Click it and you're removed immediately. You can also email us at hello@gamerswatchlist.com to request removal.

Affiliate links and third-party retailers

When you click an affiliate link on this site and visit a retailer like Amazon or Best Buy, you're leaving Gamers Watchlist and entering the retailer's website. At that point, the retailer's own privacy policy governs what data is collected about your visit and any purchase you make.

Retailers know that the referral came from Gamers Watchlist (that's how affiliate commissions work), but they don't receive any personal information you haven't directly provided to them. We don't share your name, email, or any other identifying information with affiliate partners.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data. We respect these rights regardless of where you're located.

👁️

Right to access

You can ask us what personal data we hold about you. For most visitors, the answer is: none beyond what Google Analytics collects anonymously.

🗑️

Right to deletion

You can ask us to delete any personal data we hold. For newsletter subscribers, this means removing your email from our list.

📤

Right to portability

You can ask for a copy of any personal data we hold about you in a portable format.

🚫

Right to opt out

You can opt out of analytics tracking using the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, and unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time.

✏️

Right to correct

If any personal data we hold about you is inaccurate, you can ask us to correct it.

📋

Right to complain

If you're in the EU or UK, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority if you believe we've handled your data incorrectly.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@gamerswatchlist.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

Children's privacy

Gamers Watchlist is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes that affect how we use your data, we'll notify newsletter subscribers by email. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

We don't anticipate frequent changes — this site's data practices are simple and we intend to keep them that way.
